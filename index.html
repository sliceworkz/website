How We Work

What working with us looks like

1

Introductory conversation

You tell us about one business process that frustrates you, or an existing application you want to modernize or replace. Free, about one hour.

2

Event modeling workshop

Your experts and ours shape the model together in a few hours. You don't start from a blank page: we prepare a first draft beforehand, based on what we learned and styled in your brand.

3

Realistic demo, about one week later

See what your software would actually look like. You decide on a tangible result built from that limited input, not on proposals based on theoretical schemas and plans.

4

Slice by slice

You set the priorities and can shift them at any moment. Each feature slice adds working functionality.

5

Run and evolve

We host, monitor and improve the software, and keep adding slices as your business changes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need an IT department to work with you?

No. We work directly with the business. Your IT team is welcome, but not required.

How quickly will we see results?

You see working software after the first slices, typically a few days after the briefing. In case of time pressure, go-live with the first slices can typically be within weeks already.

How is it priced?

Either a monthly fee based on parameters linked to your business (scope, usage, seats, …), or a one-off investment.

Who owns the software?

With ownership, you do from day one. With the monthly model, you can buy it out at a pre-agreed price at any time.

What if Sliceworkz stops existing?

The source code, build scripts and model are held in escrow and released to you.

Can we trust AI-generated code?

AI follows reference slices our engineers handcraft, and people review the result. Our development process is built for compliance, with security by design.

Where does our data live?

Wherever you want, including in the EU. The software runs on any infrastructure: on-premises, private cloud or a hyperscaler.

Can it connect to our existing systems?

Yes. Secure integration with your current landscape is part of every project.
